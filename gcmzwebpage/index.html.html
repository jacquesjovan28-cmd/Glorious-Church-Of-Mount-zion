Our Ministries

Ministries that help our church family grow in worship, discipleship and service.

🎵

Worship Ministry

Leads the congregation in praise and worship through music and song, helping every service become an offering of thanksgiving to God.

📖

Sunday School Ministry

Teaches children and youth the Word of God through Bible stories, songs and activities that build a strong foundation of faith.

🔥

Youth Ministry

Disciples teenagers through fellowship, Bible study and activities that help them grow in their walk with Christ and find their place in the church.

🤝

Young Adults (YA) Ministry

Gathers young adults for fellowship, discipleship and service, helping them build godly relationships as they navigate life, career and calling.
